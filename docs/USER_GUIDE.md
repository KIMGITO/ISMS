# KayKay's — User Guide

**For owners, managers, cashiers, riders and suppliers.** This guide shows how to do everyday jobs in the KayKay's app on a phone, tablet or computer. It matches app version 1.0.0.

You only see the screens and buttons your role is allowed to use, so some parts of this guide may not apply to you. If you need something you can't see, ask the Owner.

# 1. Getting started

## 1.1 Joining the shop

1. The Owner invites you from **Workers** using your email address.
2. You receive a code by email.
3. Open the app and sign up or sign in **with that same email address**, then enter the code.
4. If you are new, confirm your email with the code sent to it. You only get into the shop once your email is confirmed.

An invitation only works for the email it was sent to. If you want to use a different email, ask the Owner to send a new invitation. If the Owner cancels (revokes) an invitation before you finish, the half-made account is removed too.

Only the person who set up KayKay's can create a new business. Invited staff never see that option.

## 1.2 Signing in and out

Sign in with your email and password. If you forget your password, tap **Forgot password**; a six-digit code is sent to your login email. After five wrong tries the code stops working and you need a new one.

**Sign out** (Settings > Sign out) ends your session straight away. The app on that device can't reach the shop's data again until someone signs in.

## 1.3 The guided tour

The first time you sign in, a short tour points out the screens you can use. You can watch it again from **Settings > Help & tour**.

## 1.4 Locking a shared device

If several people use one device, set a personal PIN in **My Profile** so the app locks when you step away.

## 1.5 Suppliers

A supplier the shop invites (see 15.1) signs in with the same app but sees only their own **supplier page**: orders sent to them, bills, and **Requests** for stock. When the shop asks for stock, the supplier's phone rings; they accept or decline, and the shop is told.

# 2. Finding your way around

## 2.1 The screens

Screens are grouped the same way on every device:

| Group | Screens |
|---|---|
| Selling | Quick Sale, POS, Sales Log, Online Store, Payment Prompts |
| Customers | Loyalty, Promotions & Points, Regular Orders, Feedback |
| Stock | Inventory, Stock History, Stock Transfers, Production & BOM |
| Buying & deliveries | Buying, Deliveries |
| Money | Payments & Expenses, Petty Cash, Finance & Accounting |
| Reports | Home, Stock Report |
| Team | Shift, Workers |
| Admin | Roles, Audit Log, Business Management, Settings |
| You & the app | Assistant, Notifications, My Profile, About this app |

**On a phone** the bar at the bottom holds your most-used screens (cashiers have **Sell** in the middle; owners and managers have **Payments**). Tap **More** for the rest. **Double-tap** anywhere to hide the bar, and again to bring it back.

**On a computer** all screens are in the sidebar on the left.

If you work at more than one branch, switch branch from the business name at the top. Everything — screens, cart and figures — changes to that branch.

## 2.2 Searching

The search box at the top (or **Ctrl+K**) finds:

- screens, settings and keyboard shortcuts — type "invoice", "merge", "KRA" or "offline";
- products and customers;
- **any record by its number**: receipts (TXN-…), invoices (INV-…), quotes (QT-…), credit notes (CN-…), purchases (GRN-…, BILL-…), expenses (EXP-…), stock changes (STM-…), and customer ledger lines (LDG-…).

Picking a result opens the exact record. Most records show their number on a small chip; tap it to copy the number.

## 2.3 Messages from the app

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| A small pill that fades away | Your action worked |
| A red message | Something went wrong; read it, it says what to do |
| A **rose-red** message with a cloud | Saved on this device; it will send when the internet is back. This is not an error |
| A thin strip under the header | You are offline; it shows how many sales are waiting to send |
| A phone that rings and won't stop | A ringing alert (a reminder, a stock request, a regular order not done). Open it to stop it |

## 2.4 Numbers

Money always shows two decimals: **KSh 1,250.50**, **KSh 100.00**. Quantities have at most one decimal: **2**, **1.5**. Number boxes drop leading zeros by themselves.

# 3. Your shift

1. Open **Shift** and tap **Clock in** (or press **C**).
2. Enter the cash in the drawer when you start.
3. Every sale you make is added to your shift automatically.
4. At the end, tap **Clock out** and count the cash.
5. The end-of-shift report shows every payment type, the cash you counted against the cash expected (after your petty cash and expenses), and any difference. Add a note if the cash doesn't match.
6. The report goes to the Owner.

Your shift is the same on every device: clocking out on the phone also clocks you out on the computer.

**"Still working?"** After 15 hours on shift the app asks if you are still working. Tap **Yes** within 3 minutes, or you are clocked out automatically at the time the question came up. If you keep working, it asks again every 2 hours.

**Hours worked:** the Shift screen shows your hours by day, week and month. People who manage staff see everyone's.

**Reminders:** press **N** on the Shift screen to set a reminder. It rings at the time you choose, even if the app is closed.

# 4. Selling with Quick Sale

Quick Sale is the fastest way to sell at a busy counter.

1. Open **Sell** (Quick Sale).
2. Scan the barcode, or type the product's name or code and press **Enter**.
3. To change the amount, type it, for example **2** or **0.5** for half a kilo. **−** and **+** change it by one. You can't sell more than you have in stock.
4. Choose how the customer paid: **Cash** (Alt+1), **M-Pesa** (Alt+2), **Card** (Alt+4) or **Airtel Money** (Alt+6). Only the methods the Owner has switched on appear.
5. The sale is saved and a green banner appears.

From the banner you can:

- **Receipt** (Alt+R) to print or send a receipt;
- **Add customer** (Alt+C) so a regular customer still gets their loyalty points (section 8.4).

Press **Esc** to clear the search, and **Esc** again to clear the whole sale.

<div class="page-break"></div>

# 5. Selling with the POS

Use the POS when you need more than Quick Sale: a customer, discounts, codes, delivery, credit, quotes or split payments.

1. Open **POS** and add products by tapping them or searching (**/** or **F2**).
2. **Choose a customer** (Alt+C) if they are registered. Only registered customers earn points. Their delivery location is filled in for you.
3. Add a **discount** or change a price if your role allows it.
4. Type a **promotion or referral code** in the code box if the customer has one. The discount is checked with the shop before it is applied. Codes need the internet.
5. For **delivery** (Alt+D), choose the area, the rider (from the list or just type their name) and how the delivery is paid:

   | Choice | Who pays | The rider |
   |---|---|---|
   | **On our bill** | The fee is added to the bill (or to their account if on credit) | Collects nothing; the shop pays them |
   | **Pay the rider** | The customer pays the rider in cash | Keeps the fee |
   | **Free** | Nobody | The shop pays them |

   What the rider earns is recorded as owed to them (section 16.3).
6. Add a **Note** (Alt+N) if needed.
7. Choose the payment: **M-Pesa** is picked first. Others: **Cash**, **Card**, **Bank**, **Airtel Money**, **Mobile Wallet** (their prepaid balance) or **Credit** (section 7). A customer on weekly or monthly terms gets **Credit** picked automatically. You can split a payment between two methods.
8. If the customer has points, you can use them as a discount. The cart shows "Paid with N points" before you finish.
9. Tap **Complete** (Ctrl+Enter or F9).

**M-Pesa or Airtel prompt:** choose **Send prompt** and the customer gets a request on their phone to enter their PIN. The sale is recorded when they pay.

**Quotes:** press **Quote** (Alt+Q) to save the cart as a numbered quote (QT-…) and share it as a PDF or on WhatsApp. Quotes are listed in **Sales Log > Quotes**; tap **Sell in POS** to load one back at the quoted prices.

**VAT:** the VAT line shows the rate actually charged. VAT can only be turned on once the shop is connected to KRA eTIMS.

On a phone, **F4** or the cart button shows and hides the cart.

# 6. Receipts, refunds and sale changes

## 6.1 Sending a receipt

After a sale, or from **Sales Log**, open the receipt. The customer's phone and email are filled in if they are on file.

| Button | Key | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Print | P | Choose a printer and print |
| WhatsApp | W | Sends the receipt as a WhatsApp message |
| Email | E | Emails the receipt (as a PDF on Android) |
| PDF | D | Saves or shares a PDF |
| Copy | C | Copies the receipt text |
| Barcode | B | Shows or hides the barcode |
| Add customer | A | Puts a walk-in sale on a customer |

The receipt shows points used and earned, the customer's new balance, any wallet money used, and how delivery was paid ("Delivery: Free", "Delivery: pay the rider"). A credit sale is marked **not paid**.

If the shop is connected to KRA eTIMS, signed sales show a KRA section and QR code. A receipt never shows a KRA section unless KRA really signed that sale.

## 6.2 Refunds

A sale is never deleted to undo it. In **Sales Log**, swipe or hold the sale (or open it) and tap **Refund**. A numbered **credit note** (CN-…) is made, the stock goes back, and you choose how the customer gets their money: back in cash or M-Pesa, into their wallet, or off their debt.

Cashiers can refund their own sales on the same day. Older sales need a manager.

## 6.3 Changing a sale

Tap **Edit** on the sale. The old sale is refunded in full and its items are loaded back into the POS cart at the same prices, for the same customer. Change what you need and complete it as a new sale. Figures count only the new sale.

Every change to a sale is kept. Managers see **Edited N×** on the sale and can open the list of changes.

## 6.4 Setting up a printer

**Bluetooth printer (Android):**

1. Turn the printer on.
2. Pair it once in the phone's **Settings > Bluetooth**.
3. In the app, open **Settings > Receipts & printing**, find the printer and tap **Test print**.

**Wi-Fi or network printer (Android, Windows, Linux):** connect the printer to the same network, then find it in **Settings > Receipts & printing** and test it.

Every time you print, the app asks you to confirm the printer. In a web browser the app can't print directly; use **PDF** instead.

# 7. Selling on credit

A credit sale means the customer takes the goods now and pays later.

1. In the POS, choose the customer. Credit needs a registered customer.
2. Choose **Credit** (Alt+3). Customers on weekly or monthly terms get it picked for them.
3. Complete the sale.

If the customer has money in their wallet, the wallet pays first and only the rest becomes debt. A customer never holds wallet money and debt at the same time: a top-up pays their debt off first.

The amount owed is **not** a payment, so:

- the notification says **Sold on credit**, not "Payment received";
- the receipt is marked not paid;
- the customer earns **no points** now. They earn points as they pay the debt off.

When the customer pays, record it on their page (section 12.2). Their oldest debts are cleared first.

Credit only works while online.

# 8. Customers

## 8.1 Adding a customer

Open **Loyalty** and press **N** (or tap **Add**). Enter:

- **Full name** (one box);
- **Phone number** — any network, Kenyan or international with "+";
- email (optional);
- **delivery location**: area, estate or street, building and house number, a landmark nearby, and delivery instructions.

You can also set customer type, payment terms (weekly or monthly terms mean credit is picked at the till) and notes. Names and phone numbers are tidied up automatically. The website asks customers for the same details, so both look the same.

## 8.2 A customer's page

Tap a customer to open their page.

| Tab | Use it to |
|---|---|
| Profile | Edit their details and what messages they agree to receive |
| Money | Take a payment, top up their wallet, pay debt with money or points, send a payment prompt |
| Invoices | Make an invoice for what they owe (section 11) |
| History | See what they bought and paid |
| Ledger | See every change to their balances |
| Message | Send a WhatsApp, SMS or email |

The page also shows their **regular order** if they have one (section 10), and their referral code.

## 8.3 Messaging many customers

To message a group (for example, everyone who owes money), open **Feedback > Communication**. Pick the groups, write the message, and use variables such as **{first_name}**, **{debt}** or **{points}** to fill in each customer's own details. **Write with AI** drafts it for you to check.

## 8.4 Giving points for a walk-in sale

If you made a Quick Sale and then the customer says "I'm a member":

1. Tap **Add customer** on the sale banner (Alt+C), or open the sale's receipt and tap **Add customer** (A).
2. Choose the customer.

They get the same points as if you had chosen them at the till.

- Cashiers can do this for their own sales from the last 24 hours.
- Managers can do it for any sale from the last 30 days.
- Once a sale has a customer, it can't be moved to another customer.

## 8.5 The same customer twice

If someone was added at the shop and also signed up on the website, they appear twice. Managers can join them in **Loyalty > Duplicates**: check the details, choose which name and phone to keep, and merge. Debts, wallets, points and history are added together. **A merge can't be undone.**

# 9. Promotions & Points

**Promotions & Points** holds everything that rewards customers. Tabs: **Overview**, **Codes**, **Referrals**, **Points**.

## 9.1 How points work

| When the customer… | They earn |
|---|---|
| Pays for goods | **2 points for every KSh 1** (delivery not included) |
| Buys on credit | Nothing at the time |
| Pays off a debt | **1 point for every KSh 1** repaid |

What points are worth depends on the customer's tier:

| Tier | Reached at total spending of | Points needed for KSh 1 off |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | KSh 0.00 | 50 points |
| Silver | KSh 10,000.00 | 100 points |
| Gold | KSh 30,000.00 | 200 points |

**Points are a discount, not money.** They can't be paid out as cash, M-Pesa or wallet money. A customer can use them at the till, or on their page to pay off what they owe. They need at least 100 points first.

Owners can switch points on or off and change these values on the **Points** tab, which also has the **points report**: points held and what they're worth, earned, used, and what they cost the shop. **P** on Loyalty opens it.

## 9.2 Promotion codes

Press **N** for a new promotion. Choose:

- **what it gives**: a percentage or KSh off, or a free item;
- **what it covers**: the whole sale, chosen items or groups;
- **who** can use it (anyone or chosen customers), **when**, a minimum spend, and how many times;
- whether it works with other discounts.

The same code works at the till and on the website. Every use is recorded and counted in the books as a promotion discount.

## 9.3 Referrals

A referral campaign gives each customer a personal code to share. The friend gets a welcome discount on their first purchase; the customer who shared it gets a reward (a code or wallet money) after the friend's purchase is confirmed. Suspicious referrals wait in **To check** for a manager.

## 9.4 Advice

The **Advisor** card reads your figures and suggests what to try. It never changes anything by itself.

# 10. Regular Orders

For customers you supply on fixed days, such as daily milk.

1. Open **Regular Orders** and add one: the customer, the product, and the amount for **each day of the week** (for example 20 on Monday, 23 on Wednesday, none on Sunday).
2. Each day, the **By day** list shows who is due and how much.
3. Change one day only, or **skip** a day with a message to the customer.
4. Tap **Delivered**. One sale is made on the customer's account (wallet first, then debt).

To undo a delivery, refund the sale in Sales Log; the day goes back to "due".

Customers can ask for a regular order on the website. A manager approves it before it starts.

If today's orders aren't done by the ring time (4 pm unless changed), the phones of staff who handle them ring every 2 hours. In the evening the app warns if stock is short for tomorrow.

# 11. Invoices

An invoice is a bill for what a customer owes on credit. It is different from a receipt.

1. Open **Loyalty**, choose the customer and open **Invoices**.
2. Choose what to bill: **everything owed**, **this week**, **this month** or **your own dates**. You can include older unpaid debt.
3. Check the preview. Each credit sale is listed at the price it was sold at. You can edit the descriptions and texts.
4. Tap **Issue**. The invoice gets the next number (INV-…) and is saved.
5. Share it as a PDF, by WhatsApp or by email.

Issued invoices stay on the customer's page and can be sent again later.

Owners and managers set the invoice texts, number prefix, due days, payment details, logo size and stamp in **Settings > Invoices**.

# 12. Payments, expenses and petty cash

**Payments & Expenses** has tabs for **Payments**, **M-Pesa**, **Airtel Money**, **Cards**, **Expenses** and **Methods**. Each one shows its balance; the balances and the books always agree.

## 12.1 How customers can pay

| How the customer pays | What happens |
|---|---|
| You send a **prompt** (Payment Prompts screen, POS, or their page) | They enter their PIN; the payment is recorded against them automatically |
| They pay the M-Pesa **till** from their own phone | The payment has no account number, so it waits in **M-Pesa > Paid to the till** |
| They pay Airtel Money from their own phone | Record it by hand |
| They order on the website or by dial code | They get a prompt; the order is paid when they enter their PIN |

**Payment Prompts** (G then M) is the quickest way to ask a customer to pay: choose M-Pesa (M) or Airtel Money (A), type the phone number and amount, and watch it go from sent to paid.

## 12.2 Recording a debt payment or wallet top-up

On the customer's page, open **Money**:

- **Pay debt** takes money towards what they owe; the oldest debts are cleared first.
- **Top up wallet** adds money they can spend later (it pays any debt first).
- **Send prompt** asks them to pay from their phone.
- **Points to use for debt**: enter points and tap **Pay debt**. Only the points the debt needs are used.

## 12.3 Clearing the "Paid to the till" list

Check this list every day. For each payment, choose:

- **Record for customer**: it goes to their debt first, then their wallet;
- **Already rung up as a sale**: nothing is added twice;
- **Walk-in, not a sale**: money from someone with no account;
- **Ignore**, with a note saying why.

## 12.4 Switching payment methods on or off

Owners and admins use the **Methods** tab. A method that is off disappears from the till and the website, and prompts for it are refused. Money that still arrives that way is recorded.

## 12.5 Expenses

In **Expenses**, press **N** to record the amount, category, description and a photo of the receipt. Filter by today, this week, this month or last month.

## 12.6 Petty cash

**Petty Cash** is for money taken from the till to spend, for example buying bread for the shop. Record what you took and what it was spent on. It counts as an expense and comes off the cash expected at the end of your shift. You can remove your own entry within 24 hours.

## 12.7 Paying riders

What a rider earns is owed from the day of the delivery (section 16.3). To pay them, tap **Pay a rider** on **Petty Cash** or **Expenses**, choose the rider and the amount, and where the money came from. It clears what the shop owes them and is not counted as spending a second time.

## 12.8 Mistakes

Payments and expenses are never edited into a different amount. If one was wrong, open it and tap **Void** (or **Remove**) with a reason. The money and the books are put back, the record keeps who did it and why, and you record it again correctly.

# 13. Finance & Accounting

For owners and managers. The books fill themselves: every sale, refund, payment, expense, purchase, stock change and transfer posts an entry. You don't need to type anything for the books to be right.

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Summary | Profit, money in and out, debts and VAT for the period |
| Profit & loss | Income and spending by account |
| Balance sheet | What the business owns and owes |
| Who owes | **Customers owe us** and **We owe suppliers** |
| VAT | VAT charged and paid (only with KRA eTIMS) |
| Books | Journal, accounts and trial balance |
| Checks | Count money & stock against the books; lock & rebuild |

**Count money & stock:** count the cash or stock you really have and enter it. Any difference is posted, so the books match what is in the shop.

**Locking:** owners can lock a month once it is checked. A later change to a locked month is posted as a correction in the open month instead.

The **Advisor** card explains the figures and lists actions.

# 14. Stock

## 14.1 Adding a product

In **Inventory**, press **N** or tap **Add product**. Enter the name, category, barcode or code, unit, cost, price, opening stock and the minimum stock level. For a photo, use the **Camera** (phone), **Webcam** (computer), a file, or search for a free photo. The background can be removed with one tap.

## 14.2 Receiving, adjusting and counting stock

- **Restock** when goods arrive (or receive a purchase, section 15).
- **Wastage** for spilt, sour or damaged goods.
- **Adjust** with a reason if the number is wrong.
- **Stock count** to set the number you actually counted.

When a product runs low, staff who manage stock get a **Stock Almost Finished** alert, and an **Out Of Stock** alert at zero. The alert has an **Ask supplier** button (section 15.4).

## 14.3 Stock History

**Stock History** shows every change to stock: restocks, wastage, counts, sales, transfers and production, with who did it. Each line has a number (STM-…). Sales show their receipt number.

## 14.4 Stock Transfers between branches

A transfer is not a sale: profit doesn't change.

1. **Send** (N): choose the branch and products. Stock leaves your branch straight away and shows as **On the way**.
2. The other branch **receives** it, counting what really arrived. Only that is added.
3. If something is short, the shortage stays on the transfer and the sender is told. It can be settled later as arrived late, found, or written off.

You can cancel while it is still on the way; the stock comes back. To **ask** another branch for stock, press **A**. The **By product** tab totals transfers per product by week, month or dates.

## 14.5 Stock Report

**Stock Report** (G then G) is the daily or monthly report per product for the boss: opening stock, sales, what was added, closing stock, till money, what's not paid and pending bills. Share it on WhatsApp in one tap.

## 14.6 Production

In **Production & BOM**, a recipe (BOM) says what raw materials make one batch and how many items it gives. Starting a batch (**N**) takes the ingredients out of stock; completing it adds the finished goods. If stock is short, the app won't start the batch.

# 15. Buying and suppliers

## 15.1 Suppliers

Add or invite suppliers from **Buying > Invite a supplier**, which opens **Workers** with the Supplier role. A supplier with an email gets their own sign-in (section 1.5); one without is just kept on the list. Edit a supplier's details in **Buying > Suppliers**.

## 15.2 Buying stock

1. **Reorder** lists what is running low and how much to order. Tick lines and press **N** to draft purchases.
2. A purchase goes from draft to **Ordered**. If it is above your role's limit, it waits in **Approvals** for an owner or someone allowed.
3. When goods arrive, **receive** them (GRN-…). Stock goes up and the cost is updated.
4. Record the supplier's **bill** (BILL-…). The app flags a bill that doesn't match what was ordered and received (**Needs a check**).
5. Pay the bill from **Bills**. Send back damaged goods with a **Return** (RTS-…).

## 15.3 Bills to pay

**Bills** has one filter bar: **Not paid / Paid / All** and **Today / This week / This month / All**. It lists supplier bills and **Riders to pay** together.

## 15.4 Asking a supplier for stock

From a low-stock alert or a **Reorder** line, tap **Ask supplier**. Choose **one** supplier (the preferred one is first), the amount, when you need it (today, tomorrow, a day) and a note such as "we need 200 L milk tomorrow". The supplier's phone rings. You are told when they accept or decline. A supplier without the app gets it on WhatsApp or SMS instead.

<div class="page-break"></div>

# 16. Deliveries and riders

**Deliveries** has tabs **Requests**, **Mine**, **Trips**, **Areas & prices** and **History**.

## 16.1 For riders

1. **Requests** lists deliveries with no rider. Tap **Accept**; first come, first served. The customer's phone number shows once you accept.
2. You can give a delivery back until you start it.
3. In **Mine**, mark each delivery as started and delivered. The screen tells you what to collect from the customer and what you keep.

Riders can add delivery areas and set their prices in **Areas & prices**. New areas show on the website checkout straight away.

## 16.2 Trips

An owner or manager can join two or more deliveries into one **trip** with one rider and one price. Accepting or giving back one delivery in a trip does it for the whole trip.

## 16.3 What riders are owed

Rider pay is owed from the day of the delivery and counts as spending that day. Nothing is owed for **Pay the rider** deliveries, failed or cancelled ones. A rider doesn't need an account: the name typed at the sale is enough. See and pay what each rider is owed in **Buying > Bills > Riders to pay**, or **Pay a rider** (section 12.7).

<div class="page-break"></div>

# 17. Workers and roles

**Workers** lists your team, invitations and schedules. Invite someone with their email and role (section 1.1). Hold or swipe a person to change their role, copy their details, or remove them from the business. Hold an invitation to copy its code or revoke it.

**Roles** shows what each role can see and do. Owners can change it; the change applies at once on every device. The roles are Owner, Admin, Manager, Cashier, Rider and Supplier.

**Audit Log** shows who changed what: prices, suppliers, purchases, staff PINs and roles, and sale changes.

# 18. Online Store

**Online Store** is the staff side of the shop's website.

- **Orders**: move each order on — **Start preparing**, **Mark ready for pickup** or **Send out for delivery**, **Mark completed**.
- **Messages** and **Feedback** from website customers, **Reviews** and **Product ideas**.
- **Website**: the home banner, pages, adverts, guides, and which products are on the website or hidden.

# 19. When the internet goes off

You can keep selling.

- **Cash**, **card** and **M-Pesa sales with a code you type in** are saved on the device.
- They show in rose red with **Waiting to sync** in the Sales Log, and the strip under the header counts them.
- They send by themselves when the internet comes back or the app is opened again.

**Don't ring the sale up again.** Each sale is sent only once, even if the app retries.

These need the internet: credit sales, sales with a promotion or referral code, prompts, customers, stock changes and transfers, payments, expenses and petty cash.

# 20. Home and reports

**Home** shows how the business is doing.

- Choose the **period**: today, this week, this month or your own dates. Times are Kenya time.
- On a phone you see **Sales**, **Profit**, **Money in** and **Spending**. Swipe sideways for more.
- **Needs attention** lists things to act on, such as low stock and unpaid debts.
- Tap a chart's **Full screen** button, then turn the phone sideways to see it large.

Refunds are taken off sales. Rider pay counts as spending on the day it is owed. Debt paid with points is taken off profit, because the shop gave that value away. Home and **Finance & Accounting** read the same books, so their figures agree.

For a per-product report, use **Stock Report** (section 14.5).

# 22. Updating the app

The app checks for a new version by itself shortly after it starts, when the internet comes back, and every six hours. Updates download in the background.

- **Windows and Linux:** when the update is ready, open **About this app** and tap **Restart and install**.
- **Android:** the app opens Android's installer once by itself. Tap **Install**. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from KayKay's. If you closed it, open **About this app** and tap **Install update**.

To check by hand, open **About this app** and tap **Check for updates**. If an update fails, the app tells you and **Try again** appears there.

Version numbers started again at **1.0.0** in November 2026. Phones on the old 7.x versions still update normally.

# 24. Problems and what to do

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| I can't see a screen or button | Your role doesn't include it. Ask the Owner |
| A sale says **Waiting to sync** | You were offline. It sends when the internet is back; don't ring it up again |
| The invitation code doesn't work | Sign in with the email the invitation was sent to and confirm that email, or ask for a new invitation |
| The printer isn't listed | On Android, pair it in the phone's Bluetooth settings first; check it's on |
| A customer paid the till but it isn't on their account | Find it in **Payments & Expenses > M-Pesa > Paid to the till** and assign it |
| The prompt didn't arrive | Check the phone number and that the line has M-Pesa or Airtel Money, then send again |
| A payment method is missing at the till | It is switched off in **Payments & Expenses > Methods** |
| A code says it needs the internet | Promotion and referral codes only work online |
| **Add customer** isn't on the receipt | The sale already has a customer, wasn't paid, or is too old (24 hours for cashiers, 30 days for managers) |
| I can't change a payment's amount | Void it and record it again (section 12.8) |
| I can't turn on VAT | The shop must be connected to KRA eTIMS first |
| I was clocked out on my own | You didn't answer "Still working?" within 3 minutes. Clock in again |
| My phone keeps ringing | Open the alert (a reminder, stock request or regular order) to stop it |
| "Too many requests" | Wait a few minutes and try again |
| The phone says "App not installed" when updating | Uninstall and install the latest version by hand once |
| A shortcut does nothing | Click outside any text box, then press **?** to see your keys |

For anything else, tell the Owner what you were doing and what the message said.
