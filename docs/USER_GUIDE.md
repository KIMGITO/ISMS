# KayKay's — User Guide

**For owners, managers, cashiers and riders.** This guide shows how to do everyday jobs in the KayKay's app on a phone, tablet or computer. It matches app version 4.2.1.

You only see the screens and buttons your role is allowed to use, so some parts of this guide may not apply to you. If you need something you can't see, ask the Owner.

# 1. Getting started

## 1.1 Joining the shop

1. The Owner invites you from **Workers** using your email address.
2. You receive a code by email.
3. Open the app and sign up or sign in **with that same email address**, then enter the code.

An invitation only works for the email it was sent to. If you want to use a different email, ask the Owner to send a new invitation.

## 1.2 Signing in

Sign in with your email and password. If you forget your password, tap **Forgot password**; a six-digit code is sent to your login email. After five wrong tries the code stops working and you need a new one.

## 1.3 The guided tour

The first time you sign in, a short tour points out the screens you can use. You can watch it again at any time from **Settings > Help & tour**.

## 1.4 Locking a shared device

If several people use one device, set a personal PIN in **My Profile** so the app locks when you step away.

# 2. Finding your way around

## 2.1 On a phone

- The **bar at the bottom** holds your most-used screens. Cashiers have **Sell** in the middle; owners have **Payments**.
- Tap **More** for every other screen. The screens are grouped (Selling, Money, Stock, Team, Business, You & the app), and there is a search box at the top.
- Your profile, the light/dark theme and AI drafts are at the top of the **More** sheet.
- **Double-tap** anywhere to hide the bottom bar when you need more room, and double-tap again to bring it back. It also slides away while you scroll down a long page.

## 2.2 On a computer

All screens are in the sidebar on the left, in the same groups.

## 2.3 Searching

The search box at the top (or **Ctrl+K** on a keyboard) finds:

- products, customers and sales;
- **features**, for example type "invoice", "merge", "KRA" or "offline";
- **keyboard shortcuts**.

## 2.4 Messages from the app

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| A small pill that fades away | Your action worked |
| A red message | Something went wrong; read it, it says what to do |
| A **rose-red** message with a cloud | Saved on this device; it will send when the internet is back. This is not an error |
| A thin strip under the header | You are offline; it shows how many sales are waiting to send |
| A green bar at the top | A new version of the app is downloading |

# 3. Your shift

1. Open **Shift** and tap **Clock in** (or press **C**).
2. Enter the cash in the drawer when you start.
3. Every sale you make is added to your shift automatically.
4. At the end, tap **End shift** and count the cash.
5. The end-of-shift report shows every payment type, the cash you counted against the cash expected, and any difference. Add a note if the cash doesn't match.
6. Send the report to the Owner.

If ending the shift fails (for example, the internet drops), the report stays open with your notes. Try again when you're back online.

# 4. Selling with Quick Sale

Quick Sale is the fastest way to sell at a busy counter.

1. Open **Sell** (Quick Sale).
2. Scan the barcode, or type the product's name or code and press **Enter**.
3. To change the amount, type it, for example **2** or **0.5** for half a kilo. **−** and **+** change it by one. You can't sell more than you have in stock; the app tells you if you try.
4. Choose how the customer paid: **Cash** (F1), **M-Pesa** (F2) or **Card** (F3).
5. The sale is saved and a green banner appears.

From the banner you can:

- **Receipt** (F8) to print or send a receipt.
- **Add customer** (F7) so a regular customer still gets their loyalty points. See section 8.4.

Press **Esc** to clear the search, and **Esc** again to clear the whole sale.

# 5. Selling with the POS

Use the POS when you need more than Quick Sale: choosing a customer, discounts, delivery, credit or split payments.

1. Open **POS** and add products by tapping them or searching (**F2** or **/**).
2. **Choose a customer** (Alt+C) if they are registered. Only registered customers earn points.
3. Add a **discount** or change a price if your role allows it.
4. For delivery, turn on **Delivery** (Alt+D) and choose the area and rider. Delivery fees don't earn points.
5. Add a **Note** (Alt+N) if needed.
6. Choose the payment: **Cash**, **M-Pesa**, **Card**, **Bank**, **Mobile Wallet** (the customer's prepaid balance) or **Credit** (section 7). You can split a payment between two methods.
7. If the customer has points, you can use them as a discount. The cart shows "Paid with N points" before you finish.
8. Tap **Complete** (F9 or Ctrl+Enter).

**M-Pesa prompt:** choose **Send M-Pesa prompt** and the customer gets a request on their phone to enter their PIN. The sale is recorded when they pay.

**VAT:** the VAT line shows the rate actually charged. VAT can only be turned on once the shop is connected to KRA eTIMS.

On a phone, **F4** or the cart button shows and hides the cart.

# 6. Receipts and printing

## 6.1 Sending a receipt

After a sale, or from **Sales Log**, open the receipt. The customer's phone and email are filled in if they are on file.

| Button | Key | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Print | P | Choose a printer and print |
| WhatsApp | W | Sends the receipt as a WhatsApp message |
| Email | E | Emails the receipt (as a PDF on Android) |
| PDF | D | Saves or shares a PDF |
| Copy | C | Copies the receipt text |
| Barcode | B | Shows or hides the barcode |
| Add customer | A | Puts a walk-in sale on a customer |

The receipt shows points used and what they were worth, points earned, and the customer's new balance. A credit sale is marked **not paid**.

If the shop is connected to KRA eTIMS, signed sales show a KRA section and QR code. A receipt never shows a KRA section unless KRA really signed that sale.

## 6.2 Setting up a printer

**Bluetooth printer (Android):**

1. Turn the printer on.
2. Pair it once in the phone's **Settings > Bluetooth**.
3. In the app, open **Settings > Receipts & printing**, find the printer and tap **Test print**.

**Wi-Fi or network printer (Android, Windows, Linux):** connect the printer to the same network, then find it in **Settings > Receipts & printing** and test it.

Every time you print, the app asks you to confirm the printer. In a web browser the app can't print directly; use **PDF** instead.

# 7. Selling on credit

A credit sale means the customer takes the goods now and pays later.

1. In the POS, choose the customer. Credit needs a registered customer.
2. Choose **Credit** (Alt+3) as the payment.
3. Complete the sale.

The amount is added to what the customer owes. It is **not** a payment, so:

- the notification says **Sold on credit**, not "Payment received";
- the receipt is marked as not paid;
- the customer earns **no points** now. They earn points as they pay the debt off.

When the customer pays, record it on their page (section 10.2). Their oldest debts are cleared first.

Credit only works while online.

# 8. Customers and loyalty points

## 8.1 Adding a customer

Open **Loyalty** and press **N** (or tap **Add**). Enter their name and phone number, and optionally email, customer type, payment terms, delivery schedule and notes. Names are saved with capital letters automatically.

## 8.2 A customer's page

Tap a customer to open their page.

| Tab | Use it to |
|---|---|
| Money | Take a payment, top up their wallet, pay debt with money or points, send an M-Pesa prompt |
| Invoices | Make an invoice for what they owe |
| History | See what they bought and paid |
| Ledger | See every change to their balances |
| Message | Send a WhatsApp, SMS or email |
| Profile | Edit their details and what messages they agree to receive |

## 8.3 How points work

| When the customer… | They earn |
|---|---|
| Pays for goods | **2 points for every KSh 1** (delivery not included) |
| Buys on credit | Nothing at the time |
| Pays off a debt | **1 point for every KSh 1** repaid |

What points are worth depends on the customer's tier:

| Tier | Reached at total spending of | Points needed for KSh 1 off |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | KSh 0 | 50 points |
| Silver | KSh 10,000 | 100 points |
| Gold | KSh 30,000 | 200 points |

**Points are a discount, not money.** They can't be paid out as cash, M-Pesa or wallet money. A customer can use them:

- at the till, to take money off a sale; or
- on their page, to pay off what they owe.

A customer needs at least 100 points before using them.

## 8.4 Giving points for a walk-in sale

If you made a Quick Sale and then the customer says "I'm a member", you can still give them their points:

1. Tap **Add customer** on the sale banner (F7), or open the sale's receipt and tap **Add customer** (A).
2. Choose the customer.

They get the same points as if you had chosen them at the till.

- Cashiers can do this for their own sales from the last 24 hours.
- Managers can do it for any sale from the last 30 days.
- Once a sale has a customer, it can't be moved to another customer.

## 8.5 Points report

On **Loyalty**, press **P** or tap **Points** to see points held and what they're worth, points earned and used, who used them, and how much they cost the shop.

## 8.6 The same customer twice

If someone was added at the shop and also signed up on the website, they appear twice. Managers can join them in **Loyalty > Duplicates**: check the details, choose which name and phone to keep, and merge. Debts, wallets, points and history are added together. **A merge can't be undone**, so check carefully first.

# 9. Invoices

An invoice is a bill for what a customer owes on credit. It is different from a receipt.

1. Open **Loyalty**, choose the customer and open **Invoices**.
2. Choose what to bill: **everything owed**, **this week**, **this month** or **your own dates**. You can include older unpaid debt.
3. Check the preview. Each credit sale is listed at the price it was sold at. You can edit the descriptions and texts.
4. Tap **Issue**. The invoice gets the next number and is saved.
5. Share it as a PDF, by WhatsApp or by email.

Issued invoices stay on the customer's page and can be sent again later. The total always matches what the customer owes.

Owners and managers set the invoice texts, number prefix, due days, payment details and stamp in **Settings > Invoices**.

# 10. Taking payments and M-Pesa

## 10.1 The shop's M-Pesa till

KayKay's uses an M-Pesa **till** (Buy Goods). When a customer pays the till from their own phone, the payment carries no account number, so the app can't tell who paid. Those payments wait in a list for staff to sort out.

| How the customer pays | What happens |
|---|---|
| You send an **M-Pesa prompt** | They enter their PIN; the payment is recorded against them automatically |
| They pay the till from their own phone | The payment waits in **Payments & Expenses > M-Pesa > Paid to the till** |
| They order on the website or by dial code | They get a prompt; the order is paid when they enter their PIN |

## 10.2 Recording a debt payment or wallet top-up

On the customer's page, open **Money**:

- **Pay debt** takes money towards what they owe; the oldest debts are cleared first.
- **Top up wallet** adds money they can spend later.
- **Send M-Pesa prompt** asks them to pay from their phone.
- **Points to use for debt**: enter points and tap **Pay debt** to reduce what they owe. Only the points the debt needs are used.

## 10.3 Clearing the "Paid to the till" list

Check this list every day. For each payment, choose:

- **Record for customer**: it goes to their debt first, then their wallet;
- **Already rung up as a sale**: the sale was already recorded in the app, so nothing is added twice;
- **Walk-in, not a sale**: money received from someone with no account;
- **Ignore**, with a note saying why.

## 10.4 Expenses

In **Payments & Expenses > Expenses**, record the amount, category, description and a photo of the receipt.

## 10.5 Mistakes

Payments and expenses are never deleted. If one was wrong, open it and tap **Void** with a reason. The money is put back and the record keeps who voided it and why.

# 11. Stock

## 11.1 Adding a product

In **Inventory**, press **N** or tap **Add product**. Enter the name, category, barcode or code, unit, cost, price and the minimum stock level. For a photo, use the **Camera** (phone), **Webcam** (computer), a file, or search for a free photo.

## 11.2 Receiving, adjusting and counting stock

- **Restock** when goods arrive.
- **Wastage** for spilt, sour or damaged goods.
- **Adjust** with a reason if the number is wrong.
- **Stock count** to set the number you actually counted.

When a product runs low, staff who manage stock get a **Stock Almost Finished** alert, and an **Out Of Stock** alert when it reaches zero.

## 11.3 Stock History

**Stock History** shows every change to stock: restocks, wastage, counts, sales and production. Search, filter by type, and tap any line to see its details. Sales show their receipt number, for example **Sold — TXN-3F2A9C1E**.

## 11.4 Production

In **Production & BOM**, a recipe (BOM) says what raw materials make one unit of a product. Starting a batch (**N**) takes the raw milk and other ingredients out of stock; completing it adds the finished goods. If stock is short, the app won't start the batch.

# 12. When the internet goes off

You can keep selling.

- **Cash**, **card** and **M-Pesa sales with a code you type in** are saved on the device.
- They show in rose red with **Waiting to sync** in the Sales Log, and the strip under the header counts them.
- They send by themselves when the internet comes back or the app is opened again.

**Don't ring the sale up again.** Each sale is sent only once, even if the app retries.

These need the internet: credit sales, M-Pesa prompts, adding or editing customers, stock changes, payments and expenses.

# 13. Home figures (owners and managers)

**Home** shows how the business is doing.

- Choose the **period**: today, this week, this month or your own dates. Times are Kenya time.
- On a phone you see **Sales**, **Profit**, **Money in** and **Spending**. Swipe sideways for more.
- **Needs attention** lists things to act on, such as low stock and unpaid debts.
- Tap a chart's **Full screen** button, then turn the phone sideways to see it large. Press play to watch the figures change over the period.

Debt paid with points is taken off profit, because the shop gave that value away. Product profit uses today's prices, so treat it as an estimate.

# 14. Settings (owners and managers)

Open **Settings**. The list of sections can be collapsed to icons to give more room.

| Section | What you set |
|---|---|
| Receipts & printing | Paper size, shop details on receipts, printers, barcode, QR code, KRA eTIMS |
| Invoices | Invoice texts, prefix, due days, payment details, stamp |
| SMS, WhatsApp | Message service accounts |
| AI assistant | Which AI service to use |
| Backups | Google Sheets backup and its schedule |
| Device permissions | Camera, location, Bluetooth, notifications, storage |
| Notifications | What you get alerts about, sounds, pause |
| Help & tour | Watch the tour again |
| Sign out | Sign out of this device |

Receipt and invoice settings save by themselves.

**KRA eTIMS:** in **Receipts & printing > KRA eTIMS**, enter the KRA PIN, branch ID (00 for head office), device serial number and item classification code from your eTIMS approval, and choose how sales without VAT are reported. Use the test system first; its receipts are marked as not valid. Once connected, each sale is sent to KRA and its receipt carries the KRA section.

Loyalty point values and the website's delivery areas are set in **Online Store > Website**.

# 15. Updating the app

The app checks for a new version by itself a few seconds after it starts, when the internet comes back, and every six hours.

- **Windows and Linux:** a green bar shows the download, then the app restarts after 15 seconds. It never restarts while a sale has items; tap **Later** to wait.
- **Android:** the app downloads the update and opens Android's installer. Tap **Install**. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from KayKay's.

To check by hand, open **About this app** and tap **Check for updates**.

If your phone says **"App not installed"**, it is still on version 2.0.0. Uninstall the app and install the latest version by hand once; after that, updates work normally.

# 17. Problems and what to do

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| I can't see a screen or button | Your role doesn't include it. Ask the Owner |
| A sale says **Waiting to sync** | You were offline. It sends when the internet is back; don't ring it up again |
| The invitation code doesn't work | Sign in with the email the invitation was sent to, or ask for a new one |
| The printer isn't listed | On Android, pair it in the phone's Bluetooth settings first; check it's on |
| A customer paid the till but it isn't on their account | Find it in **Payments & Expenses > M-Pesa > Paid to the till** and assign it |
| The M-Pesa prompt didn't arrive | Check the phone number and that the line has M-Pesa, then send again |
| **Add customer** isn't on the receipt | The sale already has a customer, wasn't paid, or is too old (24 hours for cashiers, 30 days for managers) |
| I can't turn on VAT | The shop must be connected to KRA eTIMS first |
| A customer's balance change was refused | The customer was merged; close and reopen the app |
| "Too many requests" | Wait a few minutes and try again |
| The phone says "App not installed" when updating | Uninstall and install the latest version by hand once |
| A shortcut does nothing | Click outside any text box, then press **?** to see your keys |

For anything else, tell the Owner what you were doing and what the message said.
